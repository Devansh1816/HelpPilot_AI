Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What we collect

  • Store owners: email address, store name, FAQ content you add, and payment reference numbers (UTR) you submit.
  • Website visitors who use the chat widget: the messages they type and a random anonymous visitor ID stored in their browser session. We do not ask visitors for their name or contact details; they should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in chat.

How we use it

To run the chat service, show conversations and analytics to the relevant store owner, verify payments, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

Who processes data

We use trusted providers to operate the Service: Supabase (database and authentication), Vercel (hosting), Google (Gemini AI, which receives visitor messages and the store's FAQ content to generate replies), and EmailJS (transactional email notifications). We do not sell personal data.

We may send account, payment-status and support-request notifications to the relevant platform administrator or store owner. Notification emails contain only the information needed to act on the event; visitors' full chat messages are not included in escalation notification emails.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account is active. To request access, correction or deletion of your data, contact us at the address below.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is restricted per store. No system is perfectly secure.
